- the weekly health table, judged by: `crewkit health --strict` run by anyone, with every red named
- the approval pack, judged by: every item in it carries a rule-2 reason and an acceptance test

## I am owed
- a decision on an escalation, from founder, within 48h
- read access to every agent folder, at level A

## Done means
- the health table exists for the current week and every red has a named owner

## If I cannot
- hand back to founder, within 24h, recording why, as a logged decision

## Breach
- mine: no health table for two weeks -> demote to level A and raise a work item
- yours: an escalation unanswered for 48h -> the role goes amber and founder is named in the event log
