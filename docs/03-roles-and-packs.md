# Roles and packs

Start with the fewest agents that cover the work. A new agent costs attention and tokens, so it must earn its place.

## The chief operator
One agent coordinates. It:
- keeps the plan and the decision log;
- prepares one daily approval pack for the founder;
- runs the weekly health check;
- writes the weekly brief.

The founder decides; the chief operator makes deciding fast.

## Packs
| Pack | Agents | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product (one per product) | Product owner, builder, tester, support | Owner holds vision, lifecycle and kill lines |
| Growth | Growth lead, content, sales | Drafts only; the founder sends |
| Research | Scout | Hands off only with evidence of a real gap |
| Trust and risk | Guardian (security, privacy, honesty), legal register | Read-only reviews; no legal advice |
| Finance | Finance | Prepares and recommends; never pays |

## Creating an agent
1. **Check it is needed.** Could an existing agent take it with a small lane change?
2. **Write the charter** (templates/charter.md), including kill criteria. The founder approves.
3. **Make the folder:** charter, state (stage: Trial), backlog with three to five items.
4. **Write the brief:**
   - who it is and who it reports to;
   - what to read first (the protocol, its charter, state, backlog and two or three named files, not "everything");
   - its first task, with a done-test;
   - what it must never do;
   - how to end every turn.
5. **Set routines and wake-ups.**
6. **Trial for two weeks.** Then decide: active, extend or retire.

## The founder's week
- **Daily:** read the approval pack and answer yes, no or change on each item. Each item already carries evidence,
  a recommendation and the risk of saying no; if not, send it back.
- **Weekly:** the brief: health table, wins, reds, decisions, cost.

The founder never chases status, relays messages between agents, or re-explains the rules.
