# Cost hygiene

1. **Short files, read every turn:** `state.md` under about 40 lines, `backlog.md` under about 30 open items.
   Archive the rest into `archive/YYYY-MM.md`.
2. **Read what you need.** Briefs name two or three context files; no "read the whole workspace" routines.
3. **Right rhythm per agent.** Daily only where something changes daily. Weekly for research, content and legal.
   Support sleeps until there are users.
4. **Do not wake an agent to say "nothing new".** A routine that finds no change writes one line and stops.
5. **Statuses are five lines.** Long reports go in a file; the message links to it.
6. **Coding agents get one task and one acceptance test.** Vague tasks burn the most tokens. Cancel agents that loop.
7. **No duplicate work.** Check other agents' backlogs before starting research.
8. **Pause, do not delete.** A paused agent keeps its files with its routines off; it restarts from `state.md`.
9. **Kill criteria are real.** An agent at its kill line goes into the approval pack.
10. **No secrets in files or chat.** It is not a cost line, but it is the costliest mistake.
